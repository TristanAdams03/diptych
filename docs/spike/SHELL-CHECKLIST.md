# Diptych launcher checklist (v0.3.0)

On the Thor. One action per line.

If KH1 won't start or the controls stop working: GameNative → Mods → remove the mod. Nothing else changes.

This build adds Diptych's own home screen (the Shelf). The KH1 mod is the same as v0.2.1.

## Install

1. On your home Wi-Fi, open https://gitea.fantastictwo.net/joe/diptych/releases
2. Open **v0.3.0** and download `Diptych-v0.3.0.apk`.
   - You don't need the new mod zip if v0.2.1's mod is installed. The mod didn't change.
3. Install the APK.
   - If Android refuses to install over the old one: uninstall "Diptych" first, then install again.
4. Open "Diptych". It moves itself to the bottom screen.
5. If Android asks to allow notifications, tap **Allow**.
   - A quiet notification appears: "Diptych is listening for your game".

## The Shelf

6. Check the top bar: the hinge mark, **Diptych**, and the tabs Shelf · Device · Guide.
7. Check the covers: TTW, KH1 and KH2, with Steam art.
   - KH1 should say **✓ READY** (you've run the mod before).
   - TTW and KH2 say **SOON**.
8. Check the line under the top bar shows your GameNative version.
9. Tap the KH1 cover. Its name and a **Play** button appear underneath.

## The fold-out

10. With KH1 selected, tap **Details** (next to Play) to open its fold-out.
11. Check the left side shows KH1's art and the required version.
12. Check the right side shows "Before you play" with the steps ticked.
13. Tap **Show me how**. The KH1 checklist opens in the browser. Come back.

## Playing

14. Tap **Play** on the Shelf. GameNative opens on the top screen.
15. Launch KH1 and load your save.
16. Check the bottom screen switches to the KH1 map by itself.
17. Tap the small hinge tab in the top-left corner. A menu opens: Shelf, Device, Settings.
18. Tap **Shelf**. Check a "Back to game" chip shows. Tap it to return to the map.
19. Quit KH1.
20. Wait 10 seconds. Check the Shelf comes back by itself.

## Device, Guide, Settings

21. Tap **Device**. Check the GameNative version and the battery line.
    - If it says Android may stop GameNative: follow the line it shows (Settings → Apps → GameNative → Battery → Unrestricted).
22. Tap **Identify screens**. Check the bottom screen shows its number for 3 seconds.
23. Tap **Guide**. Check the KH1 and TTW guide cards open in the browser.
24. Tap the gear. Turn **Steam artwork** off. Check the covers become plain initial tiles. Turn it back on.

## Staying connected

25. With KH1 running, swipe Diptych away from recent apps.
26. Open Diptych again. Check it shows the KH1 map within about 2 seconds.

## If something looks wrong

27. On the KH1 map, tap **This looks wrong** and paste what it copied to Claude.
28. For anything on the Shelf: take a photo and describe what's wrong.

## Diagnostics (only if Claude asks)

- On the Shelf: hold the **Diptych** wordmark for one second.
- On the KH1 map: hold the top strip for one second.
- Hold again to go back.
