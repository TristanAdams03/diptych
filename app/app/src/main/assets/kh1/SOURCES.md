# KH1 map data: sources and licenses

`worlds.json` and `party.json` are generated by `tools/data/build_kh1_data.py` from the research
drafts in `docs/research/kh1-data-draft/` and the hand-authored grid in
`tools/data/layouts/kh1_layouts.json`. Do not edit them by hand: CI regenerates them and fails on a diff.

No Square Enix assets are included. Room, chest and item names are text facts. Each row's own
citation (file and line) is kept in the drafts; this page lists the repositories they come from.

## Live check

`docs/research/kh1-live-check-2026-11-04.md` (Steam 1.0.0.2, read with `tools/probe/probe.ps1`) is
authoritative for `party.json`:

- battle block `0x2D5CC4C`, stride `0x100`, 3 members in party order, u32 HP +0, max HP +4, MP +8,
  max MP +0xC: **live**;
- party slots `0x2DE97EF`, 2 bytes (slot 1, slot 2 char ids): **live**;
- the chest rule `opened = (u8[offset] >> bit) & 1`: **live** by pattern (Neverland 11/12 opened on a
  save whose farthest world is Neverland; Hollow Bastion and End of the World 0).

## What is not sourced

- **Edges** (`edges`, `edgesConfidence: "guess"`) are guesses from game knowledge. No source gives room
  links. Unknown links are left out, not invented.
- **Layout** (`col`, `row`) is hand-authored for display.
- **Neverland rooms 10–11** exist (map files `pp11`/`pp12`) but no source names them, so they are not listed.
- **Contents** are `null` for chests the randomizer source does not list (e.g. Atlantica chests/clams,
  Library carousel chests, Final Dimension 2nd/3rd/4th/7th/8th/10th).
- A chest whose draft lists several rooms (Neverland "Ship: Hold", HB Waterway, EotW Final Dimension,
  Wonderland "Bizarre Room"/"Lotus Forest", Monstro "Mouth", etc.) is put in the first one, with
  `roomConfidence: "guess"` when the sub-room is unknown.

## Confidence values

`live` (checked on the user's PC), `source` (read from the cited file), `derived` (computed from sourced
facts) and `guess`. The app treats them all the same; the field is for people.
